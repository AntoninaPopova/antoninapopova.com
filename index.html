About

I am a PhD economist with 11 years of experience in international organisations, including seven years at the United Nations in Geneva.

My work has focused on economic research, statistics and data analysis. I am now increasingly interested in what happens when economic and social data become visual: how information can be structured, explored and communicated through visualisation and design.

I am interested in the ways societies shape people — through economies, institutions, borders, culture and ideas — and in what happens when those systems change. My work explores questions about how we understand the world around us, often by looking at the gap between what we assume and what the data reveal.

This website brings together my visualisations, experiments and essays — work that sits somewhere between economics, data and information design.
