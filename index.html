About

I am a PhD economist with 11 years of experience in international organisations, including seven years at the United Nations in Geneva.

My professional background is in economic research, statistics and data analysis. Today, I combine this analytical expertise with visualisation and information design to make complex evidence more accessible, interpretable and memorable.

I am interested in the ways societies shape people — through economies, institutions, borders, culture and ideas — and in what happens when those systems change. Much of my work begins with a question about the world around us, often exploring the gap between what we assume and what the data reveal.

This website presents a selection of my independent work at the intersection of economics, data and information design.

Work With Me

I work with research-driven organisations, teams and individuals on economic and social data projects. I can support a specific stage of a project or develop it from initial data collection and analysis through to visualisation and digital publication.

01

Data collection & preparation

Finding, extracting and combining data from multiple sources. Cleaning, harmonising and structuring datasets to make them suitable for analysis and visualisation.

02

Applied research & data analysis

Exploring economic and social questions through data — identifying patterns, developing indicators, making comparisons and modelling alternative scenarios.

03

Data visualisation & information design

Designing static and interactive visualisations, maps and diagrams that make complex data easier to understand, interpret and explore.

04

Digital reports & visual storytelling

Developing standalone digital publications that combine research, analysis, editorial narrative and interactive visualisation to communicate findings to a wider audience.
